ABOUT THIS ATLAS

The map is a question,
not the final word.

Hynt traces changing political landscapes. These polygons represent approximate claimed or controlled territory. They do not describe a single ethnicity, language or belief.

Evidence & coverage

This first release includes Cliopatria polity data from 3400 BCE to 2024 CE. Blank regions are gaps in this dataset, never evidence of empty land. Confidence is not assessed by the source. Land and coastlines are present-day Natural Earth geography.
